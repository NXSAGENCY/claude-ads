# Setup Checklist

Score a trade idea one point per item met. Treat this as a default; when the
trader supplies their own checklist, use theirs instead.

| # | Item | Met when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Catalyst | News, earnings, sector move, or clear technical reason exists today |
| 2 | Relative volume | Volume is running above its usual pace for the time of day |
| 3 | Clean level | Entry sits at a level visible on the daily or pre-market chart |
| 4 | Defined invalidation | A specific price proves the idea wrong |
| 5 | Reward-to-risk | First target is at least 2R from entry |
| 6 | Liquidity | Spread is tight relative to the stop distance |
| 7 | Market alignment | Trade direction agrees with the index/sector, or the trader has a reason it need not |
| 8 | Fits the plan | The setup is one the trader has defined and journaled before |

Scoring guidance:

- 7-8: A-grade. Full planned size.
- 5-6: B-grade. Consider reduced size; name the missing items.
- 4 or fewer, or item 4 missing: pass. Say why.
