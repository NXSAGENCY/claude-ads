# Risk Rules

Default rules for when the trader has not set their own. Always prefer the
trader's stated rules.

- **Risk per trade:** 0.5%-1% of account equity. Above 2% gets a warning.
- **Daily max loss:** 2-3x the per-trade risk. When hit, the plan is done for
  the day; do not suggest new entries.
- **Max trades:** set a number before the open; stop at the limit.
- **Stops are placed before entry** and only ever moved in the trade's favour.
- **No averaging down** on a losing intraday position.
- **Size from the stop,** never from a share count chosen first.

## Tilt check

If the trader mentions revenge trading, "making it back", or raising size after
losses, pause the planning: restate the daily max loss, report how much of it
is used, and suggest stopping or cutting size in half for the rest of the day.
